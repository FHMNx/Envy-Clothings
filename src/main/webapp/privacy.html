Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how Envy Clothings collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective: 17 Dec 2025 Applies to: envyclothings.lk (and related pages)

1) Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (stored securely), phone.
  • Order data: delivery address, city, postal code, order items, totals.
  • Payment data: payment type (we do not store full card details if processed by a gateway).
  • Technical data: device/browser, IP address, basic analytics for improving the website.

2) How We Use Your Information

  • Process orders, delivery, and customer support.
  • Send order updates, invoices, and important account messages.
  • Improve site performance, security, and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse.

3) Sharing Your Information

We only share data when needed to provide the service:

  • Delivery partners: name, phone, and address for shipping.
  • Payment gateway: needed data to confirm payment status.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or valid requests.
Note: We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4) Cookies

Cookies help remember your cart/session and improve the website. You can control cookies via your browser settings.

5) Security

  • We apply reasonable security measures to protect your data.
  • Always keep your password confidential.
  • Beware of phishing messages pretending to be from us.

6) Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate details.
  • Request deletion (where legally possible).

7) Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for orders, support, accounting, and legal compliance.

8) Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact our support team using the contact page or official channels listed on the website.
